Welcome to my page!

I am currently a Clinical Research Project Manager and RA Liaison working in the Center for Brain Circuit Therapeutics at Brigham and Women's Hospital. My research interests involve brain stimulation for psychiatric illness, specialized care accessibility, and lesion network mapping techniques. I have additional years of experience working in neurodegenerative research, specifically pharmaceutical clinical trials for dementia and Parkinson's Disease.

My current plan involves applying to Clinical Psychology PhD programs to continue my work on these research interests.
